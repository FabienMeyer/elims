# Approval evidence

Approval is an explicit human decision tied to an identifiable artifact or
package revision. Completeness, status inferred from prose, an agent verdict, a
prior approval of another revision, or the absence of objections is not approval.

- Verify the artifact identity, revision, approval status, approver, and approval
  record required by the responsible skill.
- Never approve an artifact produced or reviewed by the same agent role.
- Never extend approval to added scope or a later material revision.
- When approval evidence is missing, inconsistent, or refers to another revision,
  stop before gated work and identify the exact missing evidence.
- Preserve the responsible skill's stricter entry gates and verdict vocabulary.
